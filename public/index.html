PILLAR 1: THE CORE INSIGHT

Distracted Driving Easily Outpaces Traffic Tickets

With over 24,000+ annual collisions tied to cognitive distraction, relying on traffic tickets alone will never reach Vision Zero. True traffic safety requires transforming the physical street environment so momentary driver mistakes aren't fatal.

Pillar 2: Solutions & Engineering Fixes

Engineering Self-Enforcing Streets

Because warning signs and traffic tickets cannot prevent cognitive distraction, our solutions framework focuses on physical street redesigns that force drivers to slow down and navigate carefully:

  • Protected Bike Lanes: Physical barriers separating cyclists from high-speed motor vehicles.
  • Pedestrian Refuge Islands: Concrete medians shortening crossing distances on multi-lane avenues.
  • Road Diets & Daylighting: Lane narrowing and corner bulb-outs that eliminate blind spots at intersections.
